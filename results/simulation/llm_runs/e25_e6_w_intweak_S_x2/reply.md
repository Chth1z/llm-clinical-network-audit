The network is **not a monotone clinical risk score**. Age and WFNS 4–5 generally raise its output, and treatment generally lowers it, but several effects reverse with the other inputs. For example, particular laboratory increases can sharply *reduce* predicted mortality; hypertension can also become protective in specific contexts.
